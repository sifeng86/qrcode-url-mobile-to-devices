Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or app?

No. Sendline works in the browser and does not require sign-up, login, or an app installation.

Can I send a link from my phone to my computer?

Yes. Scan the QR code, open the sender page on your phone, and send the link directly to the receiving browser.

Can I send files with a QR code?

Yes, when file storage is configured. Files are uploaded temporarily and can be downloaded on the receiving device.

Do shared items expire?

Yes. Every shared item has an availability period, and temporary file objects are deleted after expiry.
